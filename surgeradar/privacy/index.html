Privacy policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

1. Who we are

SurgeRadar is a web app and iPhone app that helps you compare Uber and Lyft and predicts where rideshare surge pricing is likely around large events. SurgeRadar is not affiliated with Uber or Lyft and has no access to their systems.

Person in charge of the protection of personal information: the SurgeRadar Privacy Officer, cadpack.support@gmail.com.

2. Your location

We ask for your location only after you tap “Use my location”, and only to show nearby zones and work out a walking route.

Your location is processed on your device. We don’t send it to our servers and we don’t store it. To show the street name of a suggested pickup spot, we send only the approximate hexagonal area of that spot (about 0.1 km²), never your position.

To find events wherever you are, the app sends only a coarse area of about 250 km² around you or the part of the map you’re looking at, never your position.

You can also set your position by hand on the map without granting location access.

3. Links to Uber, Lyft and maps

The “Check Uber”, “Check Lyft” and “Walking directions” buttons open those services with the pickup spot in the link. Once opened, that service’s privacy policy applies. These links carry no referral or affiliate codes.

4. Reports

If you choose to answer the quick questions after checking Uber or Lyft, we store your answers (which app was cheaper, whether there was surge pricing and, if you choose, roughly how much you saved or the prices you saw), the approximate hexagonal area of your pickup (never your exact GPS position), the time relative to the end of the event and the weather.

To prevent duplicates, a random device identifier is stored in hashed form. It is deleted after 90 days.

Your running total of savings is kept only on your device.

5. Account (iPhone app, optional)

You can use SurgeRadar without an account. If you choose Sign in with Apple, we receive an anonymous identifier from Apple that is specific to SurgeRadar; we store it only in hashed form. We don’t ask Apple for your name or email.

Your account stores only your savings answers and your number of reports, so your total follows you to a new phone. You can sign out or delete your account at any time in the app’s Account tab; deleting it erases this data from our servers.

6. Advertising

The iPhone app may show one banner ad and an optional “watch an ad for a week without ads” video, served by Google AdMob. The website shows no ads.

Ads are never personalized. SurgeRadar doesn’t track you across other apps or websites, doesn’t ask for tracking permission and doesn’t give Google your phone’s advertising identifier. Google chooses ads from general information such as the app and your rough region, and uses some device and ad-interaction data to count ads, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud.

How Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Whether you’re ad-free, and until when, is kept only on your phone.

7. Providers and technical data

Your device loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap and Environment and Climate Change Canada radar images directly; those services see your IP address. Our host and database provider (Railway) may keep technical logs for security.

In the iPhone app, the map, address search and nearby Metro stations come from Apple Maps: what you type in search and the area you are looking at are sent to Apple (not to us) under Apple’s privacy policy. The weather forecast comes from Environment and Climate Change Canada for the nearest city.

Some providers may process information outside Québec.

While the website is in beta it admits a limited number of new visitors a day. It remembers that you got in with one cookie that holds only today's date and expires after two days.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, by writing to the person in charge above. You can also file a complaint with the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec.

In the iPhone app, Account › Delete my trip data erases your trip answers from our server and your trip data on the phone right away.

9. Predictions only

Everything SurgeRadar shows is a prediction. We never show Uber or Lyft prices and we don’t guarantee that any zone will be free of surge pricing.

10. Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page, with its date.
