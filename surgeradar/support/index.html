Send us feedback

Testing the iPhone app through TestFlight? Take a screenshot while you’re in SurgeRadar and tap Share Beta Feedback, or open TestFlight, choose SurgeRadar and tap Send Beta Feedback. We read every message.

You can also email cadpack.support@gmail.com.

The price in Uber or Lyft is different

SurgeRadar shows predictions, not live prices. The price in the Uber or Lyft app is the one that counts.

My location is wrong

Drag the pickup dot or tap the map to set your pickup by hand. To turn location access on or off, go to Settings › SurgeRadar › Location on your iPhone.

An event is missing

Events come from ticketing sites such as Ticketmaster, within about 30 km of you. Smaller shows that aren’t listed there won’t appear.

Account and your data

You don’t need an account. If you signed in with Apple, you can sign out or delete your account in the Account tab. The privacy policy explains what we store.
